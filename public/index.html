Features

What StackBot does today

Live Risk Scoring

Every token shows a transparent risk score before you buy — liquidity depth, token age, DEX coverage and trading volume, pulled live from mainnet. Low, Medium or High, with every input shown so you can see exactly why.

Instant Buy & Sell

Swap any SIP-010 token from Telegram. Paste a contract address or choose from popular tokens. Execution currently runs on testnet ahead of a mainnet security audit.

Best Price Routing

Quotes are fetched from ALEX, Velar and Bitflow simultaneously. Your trade always goes to the best available price.

Portfolio Tracker

View your STX and SIP-010 token balances with live USD valuation. Tracks your full wallet without leaving Telegram.

Non-Custodial

Your private key is AES-256-GCM encrypted and never leaves your database. StackBot never holds your funds.

How it works

Up and trading in three steps

1

Open the bot

Start a chat with @stackbot_test_bot on Telegram. A Stacks wallet is created for you automatically.

2

Deposit STX

Send testnet STX to your bot wallet address. Use the Stacks testnet faucet to get funds.

3

Trade

Use /buy or /sell with any SIP-010 contract address. Review the live risk score, confirm the quote, and your swap broadcasts on the Stacks testnet.
